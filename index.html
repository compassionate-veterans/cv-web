Who we are

Our mission is deeply rooted in hope and healing. For over a decade, we have facilitated over 1,500 social support groups for military veterans across California while redistributing hundreds of pounds of donated plant medicine at no charge to veterans in need.

Our diversity is our core strength. Among our family are veterans of color, 2SLGBTQIA+, and seniors, all seeking—and finding—solace and camaraderie within our unique community.

What we do

Our initial intention—to prevent veteran suicide—has revealed a protocol that works, the impact of which is tangible and humbling. In providing a platform for peer interaction with guided discussion and meditation, veterans can come together regularly and reliably, share experiences and insights, and inspire each other to grow.

Each week, we gather online to set goals, share vulnerabilities, discuss strengths and challenges, build resilience, and promote peace in somatic, iRest yoga nidra meditation.

Compassionate Veterans gather together in person each quarter to provide community service and receive donated plant-medicine products for free.
